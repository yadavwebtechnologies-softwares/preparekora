About preparekora.in

A structured place to practise MCQs, explore subjects and prepare for competitive examinations.

Our Purpose

Preparation Made More Organised

preparekora.in is designed to bring subject-wise practice, mock tests, daily challenges and current affairs categories into one consistent learning experience.

Our focus is to help learners find relevant practice sections, answer questions and review their understanding through a clear, easy-to-navigate interface.

Our Approach

Learn. Practise. Review.

An effective study routine includes revising concepts, answering questions and learning from mistakes. preparekora.in's learning sections are organised around this approach.

How to Use preparekora.in

Follow a simple path through the preparation sections.

01 Choose an Exam or Subject Browse by examination category or subject.
02 Start Practising Open a practice section and answer the available MCQs.
03 Review Your Answers Review explanations where provided and revisit weak topics.

Important to Know

Available features depend on the content and integrations configured for each section. Sample questions should not be treated as official examination papers.

Always verify examination notifications, eligibility requirements and official dates through the relevant examination authority.
