About studyKora

A structured place to practise MCQs, explore subjects and prepare for competitive examinations.

Explore Subjects Browse Mock Tests
Our Purpose

Preparation Made More Organised

studyKora is designed to bring subject-wise practice, mock-test pages, daily challenges and current affairs categories into one consistent learning experience.

The focus is on helping learners find the right practice section, answer questions and review their understanding without navigating a confusing interface.

Our Approach

Learn. Practise. Review.

A useful study routine involves revising concepts, answering questions and reviewing mistakes. studyKora's page structure is built around this sequence.

How to Use studyKora

A simple path through the preparation sections.

01 Choose an Exam or Subject Browse by organization, examination or subject.
02 Start Practising Open a practice entry and answer the available MCQs.
03 Review Your Answers Check explanations where provided and revise weak topics.

Important to Know

The current frontend includes sample questions and demonstration functionality. Exam-specific question banks, live current affairs, account authentication, saved progress and leaderboard data require further configuration and integration.

Do not treat sample tests as official exam papers or rely on unverified exam details.
