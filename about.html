About PrepareKora.in

A free mock-test platform built for Assam's competitive exam aspirants.

What we do

Free mock tests, no strings attached

PrepareKora.in is an independent, free mock-test platform created for students preparing for Assam's competitive examinations — ADRE, Assam Police, SSC, Railways, and similar government recruitment tests. Our goal is simple: give every student, regardless of coaching budget, access to quality practice material in Assamese and English.

How it works

Teachers publish, students practise

Independent teachers — we call them Publishers — create and publish mock tests on this platform. Every test is their own work. We host it, deliver it to students, and store results so the Publisher can review how students performed.

Students open a test, enter a name and (optionally) a WhatsApp number, answer multiple-choice questions on a timer, and receive an instant score with a downloadable result card.

Assamese-first

Built for both mediums

Over 80% of our audience studies in Assamese medium. Every test can be viewed in Assamese or English. Test content is authored in English and automatically translated on demand — because no student should be held back by language.

Under the hood

Lightweight, mobile-first

The site is built to load fast on low-bandwidth connections and low-end mobile devices. No heavy frameworks, no unnecessary scripts — just plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, served from a fast global edge network.

Honest limitations

Practice, not prediction

We do not claim any test is 100% exam-ready. Questions may occasionally be incorrect, outdated, or ambiguous — that's the nature of a community-published platform. Please cross-verify any fact before relying on it for your actual examination. See our Disclaimer for details.
