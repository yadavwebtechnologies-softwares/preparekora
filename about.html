About

PrepareKora.in — free mock tests for competitive exam preparation.

PrepareKora.in is a simple, free platform where students can practice mock tests for competitive exams. Every test is created by an experienced teacher and published directly by them.

Each test includes a timer, question navigation, mark-for-review and a downloadable result card so you can track your progress over time.

The site is available in English and Assamese. Content created in English is translated automatically, so Assamese-medium students can prepare in their own language.
