2. Privacy Policy

2.1 Information We Collect

When you take a mock test, we collect:

  • Your name — required, used to identify you on your result card.
  • Your WhatsApp number — optional, used only if the Publisher needs to contact you.
  • Test answers and score — used to calculate and display your result.
  • Time taken and timestamp of submission.
  • Test title you attempted.

We do not collect your email, address, payment information, or any other personal data beyond what is listed above.

2.2 Why We Collect It

  • To calculate and display your test result.
  • To generate your result card (which includes your name).
  • To allow the Publisher of that test to view student performance.
  • To help Publishers improve their tests based on aggregate data.

2.3 Who Can See Your Data

  • You — via the result screen immediately after submission.
  • The Publisher of that test — via an admin dashboard, they can see your name, WhatsApp (if provided), score, and submission time.
  • PrepareKora.in administrators — for operational support.

Your data is not visible to other students, not publicly searchable, and not shared with any third party.

2.4 How Data Is Stored

Data is stored in Firebase Realtime Database (Google Cloud). All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Only authenticated administrators can access the raw data.

2.5 Data Retention

Submissions are retained as long as the corresponding test exists. Each test stores up to the most recent 500 submissions; older submissions are automatically deleted.

2.6 No Selling or Sharing

We never sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing, advertising, or any commercial purpose.

2.7 Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to remember your language preference and to cache test questions. We do not use tracking cookies, ad networks, or third-party analytics.

2.8 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of your stored data.
  • Request deletion of your submissions.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@preparekora.in with your name and the test you attempted. We will respond within 7 working days.

Students under 18 should use the Website with parental or guardian consent. If a parent believes their child has submitted personal data without consent, they may contact us for deletion.
