No Refund Policy

This policy explains the refund conditions applicable to Premium Mock Test Plans purchased on PrepareKora.in. Please read it carefully before completing your purchase.

Digital Service

Premium Mock Test Access

PrepareKora.in offers paid digital subscriptions that provide access to available mock tests on the platform. Depending on the selected plan, students receive unlimited attempts during the applicable subscription period.

These are time-based digital access plans, not physical products. Once premium access has been successfully activated, the purchased subscription is generally non-refundable.

Refund Policy

All activated Premium Plans are non-refundable

We do not ordinarily offer refunds for successfully activated Premium Plans. A purchase provides access to the platform's available mock tests for the selected validity period, whether or not the student chooses to use the available attempts.

Students are advised to review the plan duration, price and benefits carefully before making payment.

Non-Refundable Situations

When refunds are not ordinarily provided

Once a Premium Plan has been activated, refunds are not ordinarily available in the following situations:

  • Change of mind after purchasing a Premium Plan.
  • Accidental selection of a different subscription duration or plan.
  • Not using the available mock tests during the subscription period.
  • Unused days or remaining validity after subscription expiry.
  • Fewer mock test attempts than expected.
  • Personal scheduling issues or lack of time for preparation.
  • Not achieving the expected mock test score or performance.
  • Not qualifying for or being selected in a competitive examination.
  • Requesting cancellation simply because the purchased plan is no longer needed.

Subscription Validity

Your access is limited to the selected plan duration

Every Premium Plan has a fixed validity period. The available subscription durations are:

  • Starter Plan — 7 days.
  • Standard Plan — 30 days.
  • Pro Plan — 90 days.

Unlimited attempts mean that students can attempt available mock tests repeatedly while their Premium Plan remains active. It does not mean lifetime access or unlimited subscription duration.

An expired plan does not automatically qualify for a refund, extension or restoration of unused validity.

Payment Issues

Failed or duplicate transactions

Payment-related problems are treated separately from ordinary refund requests.

  • Payment deducted but Premium access not activated.
  • Duplicate payment for the same subscription purchase.
  • Payment marked successful by the payment provider but not reflected in the user's account.

In these situations, students should contact our support team and provide the transaction reference and relevant payment confirmation. We will verify the transaction and investigate the issue.

Verified duplicate charges and eligible failed transactions will be addressed through the appropriate refund or payment-resolution process, in accordance with applicable laws and payment provider procedures.

Technical Issues

Problems accessing Premium Mock Tests

We aim to keep PrepareKora.in accessible and functional for students. However, temporary technical interruptions, maintenance or connectivity problems may occasionally occur.

If a student cannot access the purchased Premium features because of a technical problem attributable to our platform, the student should contact support with the relevant account and transaction details.

We will investigate and attempt to resolve the issue. Where the service is not delivered as agreed and the problem cannot be appropriately resolved, any applicable remedy will be considered under the relevant consumer protection requirements.

Mock Test Content

Practice material and examination outcomes

PrepareKora.in is a mock-test and examination practice platform. Mock tests are intended to help students practise, revise and evaluate their preparation.

We do not guarantee that attempting mock tests will ensure examination qualification, a particular score, selection or recruitment.

Test performance, personal expectations or the absence of a particular examination outcome alone do not qualify for a refund. Content-related concerns can be reported for review, and genuine service deficiencies will be handled appropriately.

Consumer Rights

Exceptions under applicable law

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude, restrict or override any consumer rights or remedies available under applicable Indian laws.

Where a refund or another remedy is required under applicable consumer protection laws, or where an eligible payment issue is verified, the applicable legal requirements will take precedence over this general no-refund policy.

Customer Support

Need assistance?

For payment verification, Premium access problems, technical complaints or questions regarding this policy, contact our support team.

When contacting us, please include your registered account details, transaction reference and a brief description of the issue.
