Refund Policy

Important information about payments for Premium access and eBooks.

Related pages: Terms of Service · Privacy Policy · Disclaimer

Effective date: 26 November 2026

1. Scope of this policy

This Refund Policy applies to paid Premium access plans and eBook purchases offered through PrepareKora. Free mock tests do not require payment and are not covered by this policy. Plan names, prices, benefits, and validity periods may change and are confirmed at the time of the request.

2. How payments and requests work

Premium requests and eBook purchases are handled manually through WhatsApp. PrepareKora does not currently use an integrated online payment gateway for these purchases. When you choose a Premium plan, the Website may prepare a WhatsApp message containing request details such as your name and email. You must send that message yourself and follow the payment instructions confirmed by the administrator. For eBooks, the purchase request and payment instructions are also handled through WhatsApp. Any available eBook sample is optional and is not a required step.

A pre-filled message is not sent automatically, does not itself complete a purchase, and is not proof that payment has been made. Before paying, check the plan or eBook, price, benefits, validity or other purchase details communicated to you.

3. Premium payment verification and activation

Premium access is not activated automatically when a WhatsApp message is generated or when you initiate a payment. An administrator must manually verify the payment and approve the request. Activation may take time and may not be instant. The applicable Premium benefits and validity are those confirmed for the plan approved by the administrator. Do not assume that Premium access has started until you receive confirmation and the access is visible in your account.

4. Premium payments — no routine refunds

Once a Premium payment has been made, it is not refundable merely because you change your mind, decide not to use the service, use only part of the validity period, do not use the available test access, or change your preparation plans. Please review the plan details, price, benefits, and validity confirmed to you before making payment.

This no-refund rule does not remove any refund, correction, or other remedy that may be required under applicable consumer-protection law. Payment or activation complaints will be reviewed under the process described in this policy.

5. eBook purchases — no routine refunds

Once payment for an eBook has been made, it is not refundable merely because you change your mind, no longer want the eBook, or decide not to use it. Check the eBook title, price, description, and any sample made available before completing payment. An available sample is optional.

This policy does not restrict any remedy that must be provided under applicable law, including where a proven issue concerns non-delivery, material mismatch, or deficiency in the service.

6. Incorrect, duplicate, or unverified payments

If you believe that you paid the wrong amount, made a duplicate payment, or that a payment has not been correctly identified, contact us by email. Include the purchase type, the plan or eBook involved, the date and amount of payment, and available transaction reference or proof. Do not include passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, or other security credentials in your message.

We will review the information and check the payment with the available records. Access will be granted only after the payment is verified. Any correction, reversal, or refund will be handled where required by applicable law or otherwise confirmed after review.

7. Activation, access, or delivery concerns

If your payment has been made but Premium access is not activated, or you encounter an issue with an eBook purchase, contact us with your purchase details and payment reference. We will review the request and, where appropriate, take corrective action or provide another remedy in accordance with applicable law. Manual verification can cause a delay, so a short wait for confirmation may be necessary; however, this does not limit any rights you have under applicable law.

8. Consumer rights

Nothing in this Refund Policy excludes, restricts, or waives a consumer right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded, restricted, or waived. Where a refund request is accepted or a refund is otherwise required under applicable law, it will be processed within the period and through the method required by the applicable rules. This policy must be read together with the Terms of Service and other applicable laws of India.
