Privacy Policy

How we collect, store, and use your information.

Related pages: Disclaimer · Terms of Service

Effective date: 1 November 2026

1. What we collect

  • Name — mandatory before starting a test. We do not verify whether the name is correct or belongs to you.
  • WhatsApp number — optional. If provided, stored for contact regarding your mock-test experience only.
  • Test answers — the options you select for each question.
  • Score, time taken, submission timestamp, test title.

We do not collect your email, address, payment details, or any personal data beyond the above.

2. How data is stored

All data is stored in Firebase Realtime Database (Google Cloud) and accessed only through our secure Cloudflare backend. All transmissions use encrypted HTTPS. Only authenticated administrators can access raw data.

3. Why we collect it

  • To calculate and show your result.
  • To generate your result card (which includes your name).
  • To let the Publisher of that test view student performance.
  • To contact you only regarding your mock-test experience — for example, if you reported an issue with a question, or we need feedback to improve quality.

We never use your name or WhatsApp number for marketing, advertising, or commercial purposes.

4. Who can see your data

  • You — via your result screen after submission.
  • The Publisher of that specific test — via their admin dashboard. They can see your name, WhatsApp (if provided), score, per-question answers, and submission time.
  • PrepareKora.in administrators — for operational support and quality review.

Your data is not visible to other students, not publicly searchable, and not shared with any third party.

5. Data retention

Submissions are kept as long as the corresponding test exists. Each test stores up to 500 most-recent submissions; older submissions are automatically deleted.

6. No selling or sharing

We never sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with any third party for any commercial purpose.

7. Browser storage

We use browser localStorage to remember your language preference, cache translated content, track anti-cheat interruption state during a test, and — for administrators only — store login session tokens. We do not use tracking cookies, ad networks, or third-party analytics.

8. AI-generated explanations and hints

Some post-result explanations and in-test hints are generated using an AI service. When you view an explanation or hint, we send the relevant question text and options to the AI service to produce the response. We do not send your name, WhatsApp number, or any personal information in this process.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of your stored data.
  • Request deletion of your submissions.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email yadavwebdeveloper@gmail.com with your name and the test you attempted. We will respond within 7 working days. Once deleted, submissions cannot be recovered.

10. Children's privacy

This service is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not collect or verify age at signup. If a parent or guardian believes a minor has submitted personal data, they may contact us and we will delete it on request.

11. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data — server-side authentication, encrypted transmission, and restricted access. No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be indicated by updating the effective date at the top of this page.

13. Contact

For any privacy-related complaint or data deletion request:
Email: yadavwebdeveloper@gmail.com
