Privacy Policy

How we collect, store, and use your information.

Related pages: Disclaimer · Terms of Service

Effective date: 26 November 2026

1. What we collect

  • Guest test details — a name is required before a non-login user starts a free test; an optional WhatsApp/mobile number may also be provided. We do not verify the name.
  • Account details — registered users provide their name, email address, mobile/WhatsApp number, and date of birth (DOB). DOB is checked for the 18+ account eligibility requirement. Password authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication; passwords are not stored in our Realtime Database.
  • Test information — selected answers, score, test title, time taken, submission date and time, and information needed to show the result.
  • Premium and eBook requests — where recorded for account administration, this may include the selected plan or eBook, request/approval status, and access validity. Requests and payment verification are handled manually through WhatsApp, not through an integrated payment gateway on the Website.

If you choose to send payment screenshots, transaction references, or other payment information through WhatsApp, that information is handled by WhatsApp and the relevant payment service. Do not send passwords, PINs, or banking security credentials.

2. How data is stored

PrepareKora uses Firebase Realtime Database for relevant account and test records and Firebase Authentication to manage account sign-in. Cloudflare Workers support backend requests and access controls, including restricted administrator operations. Firebase/Google and Cloudflare may process information as service providers to operate these features. Data is transmitted over HTTPS where supported by the relevant service. Access to administrative records is restricted to authorised administrator accounts.

3. Why we collect it

  • To create and manage accounts, check the 18+ eligibility requirement, and provide account settings.
  • To conduct mock tests, calculate and display scores, and generate downloadable result images showing the user's name and score.
  • To operate the test and administration systems, review submissions, provide support, manage Premium or eBook requests and access validity, and contact you about a test issue, feedback, or a request you initiated.

We do not use your name or WhatsApp number for marketing or advertising. Optional WhatsApp details are used for communication related to the service, such as test issues, feedback, or a request you initiated.

4. Who can see your data

  • You — after submitting a test, both guest and registered users can view the result screen. A downloadable result image displays the user's name and score. Access to older result/history information depends on the features available in the account.
  • Authorised PrepareKora administrators — currently the operator/admin is Yadav Subba. Authorised admin access is used for test operations, account support, submission review, and Premium/eBook administration. There are no independent teacher-publishers with separate access to student data.
  • Service providers — Firebase/Google, Cloudflare, WhatsApp, the relevant payment service, and the configured AI service may process information needed for the functions described in this policy.

Test submissions are not publicly searchable or available to other students through the Website. If you download or share your result image, the name and score shown in that image may be seen by anyone you share it with.

5. Data retention

Test submissions are retained while the corresponding test and its records remain in the database. There is currently no automatic 500-submission limit and no automatic deletion of older submissions. When an administrator deletes a test, the associated test data and submissions are deleted from the Firebase Database. Registered users can delete their account through Student Dashboard → Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account by entering their current password and confirming the action. This process deletes the Firebase Authentication account and the account data associated with it in our database. It does not delete copies already downloaded or shared by you, WhatsApp conversations, or records independently retained by payment or service providers.

6. No selling or sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data, and we do not share it with unrelated parties for advertising or marketing. This does not prevent service providers such as Firebase/Google, Cloudflare, WhatsApp, payment services, or the configured AI service from processing information needed to provide their respective services. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the Website, its users, or legal rights.

7. Browser storage

The Website may use browser storage for your language preference, cached translated content, test interruption/anti-cheat state, preload or site-functionality data, and administrator session information. We do not intend to store confidential student records in browser storage. We also use a self-developed page-visit counter to understand visits to the Website. We do not use Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager, and we do not use advertising networks for this visit-counting feature.

8. AI-generated explanations and hints

Some post-result explanations and in-test hints are generated using an AI service configured through our backend. The relevant question text and options are sent to that service to generate a response. As currently configured, PrepareKora does not send your name, email address, WhatsApp number, or DOB as part of that request, and the prompts and AI responses are not stored in our PrepareKora database. The AI provider is selected through backend configuration and may have its own processing, retention, and privacy terms. We do not control the provider's independent data-handling practices.

9. Your rights

You may:

  • Request a copy of personal information associated with your account or test submission.
  • Request correction or deletion of personal information, subject to records that must be retained under applicable law.
  • Withdraw a consent or request the deletion of information where the processing is based on that consent, subject to applicable legal requirements.

You can update your name, mobile/WhatsApp number, and DOB through Student Dashboard → Settings. Email changes are not currently supported. You can delete your registered account through Student Dashboard → Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account by entering your current password and confirming. For other privacy requests, email yadavwebdeveloper@gmail.com with enough information for us to identify the relevant account or test. Do not include your password or other security credentials in your email.

10. Children's privacy

Registered account creation is restricted to people aged 18 or above. The system checks DOB during registration and checks it again if the DOB is changed; an under-18 user cannot create or retain an eligible account. The guest free-test flow currently asks for a name and an optional WhatsApp/mobile number but does not collect DOB, so age is not independently verified in that flow. PrepareKora is intended for adults, and minors should not submit personal information through the Website. A parent or guardian who believes a minor has submitted personal information may contact us using the email below.

11. Data security

We use measures described in this policy, including Firebase Authentication, Cloudflare Worker access controls, HTTPS transmission, and restricted administrator access, to help protect information. No website, network, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should keep your account password confidential and notify us if you suspect unauthorised access to your account.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Website, its features, service providers, or applicable legal requirements change. The latest effective date will be displayed at the top of this page. You should review this page periodically for updates.

13. Contact

For privacy-related complaints, account-data requests, or deletion requests:
