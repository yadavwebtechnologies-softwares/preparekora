Privacy Policy

Information about the data studyKora may collect, how it may be used and how privacy is handled.

About This Policy

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how studyKora is intended to handle information when people visit the website, use study resources or interact with account-related features.

Some account and database features are still under development. This policy describes the intended handling of information and must be updated to reflect the actual services, providers and data practices before those features launch.

1. Information You Provide

If account registration is enabled, the information requested may include:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your WhatsApp number, including country code.
  • Authentication information handled by the selected authentication provider.

Do not send passwords, verification codes or other confidential authentication information to support contacts or other users.

2. Practice and Usage Information

If practice tracking is implemented, the platform may process information such as:

  • Questions attempted and selected answers.
  • Test scores, accuracy and completion status.
  • Subject preferences and learning progress.
  • Leaderboard information, if that feature is enabled.

Current frontend demonstrations may calculate scores within the browser. Persistent account history is not available unless the corresponding backend functionality is implemented.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used, where the relevant features are enabled and there is an appropriate legal basis, to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and support account recovery.
  • Display test results and learning progress.
  • Maintain site functionality and troubleshoot errors.
  • Protect the website against misuse and security threats.
  • Respond to privacy or account-related requests.

4. Authentication and Passwords

The planned authentication system may use Firebase Authentication. If implemented, passwords should be handled by the authentication provider rather than stored as readable text in the application database.

Password recovery must use a secure, verified process. A WhatsApp number or email address entered into a form is not, by itself, proof of account ownership.

5. Database and Service Providers

The planned application may use Firebase services for authentication and database functionality. The exact services, data fields, access controls and applicable provider policies must be confirmed before deployment.

Information should only be shared with service providers when necessary for the operation, security or support of the platform, subject to applicable law and appropriate safeguards.

6. Cookies and Browser Storage

The website may use necessary browser storage or similar technologies if they are implemented to maintain settings, session state or functionality. Any additional analytics or tracking technologies should be disclosed before use.

You can review your browser settings to manage site data. Blocking storage may affect some functionality if a feature depends on it.

7. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards should be used to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the stated purpose, applicable legal obligations and legitimate security needs. The actual retention periods must be defined when the backend is configured.

8. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law and the services available, you may be able to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, or raise an objection or other privacy request.

Requests should be reviewed through a verified process to protect the account holder and other users. Applicable legal requirements and exceptions may affect how a request is handled.

9. Children's Privacy

If the platform is used by children or minors, age-appropriate safeguards and any required parental consent processes must be implemented where applicable. Do not provide unnecessary personal information through the website.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised when features, service providers or data practices change. The updated version should be published on this page with an appropriate effective date.

11. Contact and Privacy Requests

A verified privacy contact method should be provided here before public launch. Until that contact is configured, avoid submitting sensitive personal information through unverified channels.
